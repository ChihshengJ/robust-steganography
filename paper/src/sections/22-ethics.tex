Prior work testifies to the dual-use nature of text steganography, with some emphasizing its ability to facilitate communication between private citizens under authoritarian rule \citep{perry2025robust}, while others have framed it as a means by which LLMs might render their reasoning and communications opaque to external (human or machine) monitors \citep{lanham2023measuring, roger2023preventing, skaf2025large}. Accordingly, methods like ours that aim to render text steganography more robust are likewise dual-use.
